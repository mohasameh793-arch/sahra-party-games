# Party Games App — Blueprint (draft v1)

## How the app works (for everyone)
1. **Host** taps "Create room" → picks **Same room** or **Everyone at home** → gets a **4-digit code** (e.g. 4821).
2. **Friends** tap "Join", type their name + the code.
3. Host sees "Ahmed wants to join" → taps **Accept** or **Reject**.
4. Host picks a category (**Egyptian 🇪🇬** or **Saudi 🇸🇦**) → picks a game → taps Start.
5. **Every phone shows a "How to play" card** for that game (3–5 short lines + one example). Each player taps **"I understand"**. The game starts only when everyone has tapped it (the host can skip).
6. The app is the narrator. Nobody has to sit out to run the game.

Same room = you talk face to face. Phones show secrets, timers and voting, and one phone can read the narration out loud.
Everyone at home = same game, plus the app connects people so they can talk (your choice, see the questions).

## 🇪🇬 Egyptian games

### 1. المافيا (Mafia) — 5 to 20 players
**In one line:** A few players are secretly Mafia. Everyone else must find them before the Mafia kills the town.

**What each player sees at the start:**
- **Mafia:** "You are MAFIA 🔪. Your partners are: Sara, Omar." (Mafia players know each other.)
- **Doctor:** "You are the DOCTOR 💉. Every night, pick one person to save."
- **Detective:** "You are the DETECTIVE 🔍. Every night, pick one person and the app tells you if they're Mafia."
- **Citizen:** "You are a CITIZEN 👤. You have no power. Talk, watch, and vote smart."
- Nobody sees anyone else's role.

**How a round goes:**
1. 🌙 **Night:** "Everyone close your eyes." The Mafia's phones show the player list and they pick who to kill. The Doctor picks who to save. The Detective picks who to check. Everyone else's phone just shows "Sleeping…" (so nobody can tell who is doing something from the screen).
2. ☀️ **Morning:** The app announces "Last night, Ali was killed" or "Nobody died, the Doctor saved them!"
3. 🗣️ **Talk** (timer, e.g. 3 min): everyone argues about who looks like Mafia.
4. 🗳️ **Vote:** each phone picks one name. The player with the most votes is out and their role is shown.
5. Repeat until **all Mafia are out (town wins)** or **Mafia equal the rest (Mafia wins)**.

**Rules the app handles:** the Doctor can't save the same person two nights in a row; dead players watch but can't talk or vote; a tie in the vote means nobody goes out.

**Roles per player count:** 5–6 players → 1 Mafia + Doctor + Detective · 7–9 → 2 Mafia + Doctor + Detective · 10–13 → 3 Mafia · 14–20 → 4 Mafia. The host can change these.

### 2. أتوبيس كومبليت (Autobus Complete) — 2 to 12 players
**In one line:** The app picks a letter, and everyone races to write a word for each category that starts with it.

**What each player sees at the start:** "The letter is **م**" plus empty boxes: Boy name · Girl name · Animal · Plant · Object · Country · Food.

**How a round goes:**
1. Everyone types as fast as they can.
2. The first player to fill every box taps **"أتوبيس كومبليت!"**, and everyone's boxes lock 3 seconds later.
3. Every answer is shown, and players can tap 👎 on a wrong or fake word (majority decides).
4. **Points:** your word is unique = 10 · someone else wrote the same word = 5 · empty or wrong = 0.
5. Play 5 letters. The highest total wins.

### 3. كلمة سر (Secret Word) — 4 to 12 players, 2 teams
**In one line:** One teammate sees a secret word and gives **one-word clues** until the team guesses it.

**What each player sees at the start:**
- **Clue giver:** "The secret word is **بحر**. Give ONE-word clues. Don't say the word or part of it."
- **Teammates:** "Your clue giver knows the word. Listen and guess!"
- **Other team:** "Watch. If they say a forbidden word, tap 🚫."

**How a round goes:**
1. The clue giver says one word ("سمك"), and the team guesses out loud.
2. A guess on the first clue = 10 points, the second clue = 9, and so on.
3. If the team gives up or the timer runs out, the other team gets a turn on the same word.
4. The clue giver changes every round. The first team to 50 points wins.

### 4. صراحة (Honesty) — 3 to 15 players
**In one line:** The app asks everyone the same honest question, and answers are shown **without names**.

**What each player sees at the start:** "Answer honestly. Nobody will see which answer is yours." Then a question such as: "Who in this room would survive longest on an island?" (pick a player), or "What's the funniest thing that happened to you this year?" (type it).

**How a round goes:**
1. Everyone answers privately.
2. All answers appear on every phone, shuffled and with no names.
3. Optional mini-game: everyone guesses who wrote each answer (1 point per correct guess).

**Safety:** answers are wiped after the game, and the host can remove any answer before it's shown. The question packs stay friendly (no insults or private-life questions).

## 🇸🇦 Saudi games

### 5. الذيب (The Wolf) — 5 to 20 players
**In one line:** Same idea as Mafia but Saudi style: the wolves (الذيابة) eat a villager every night, and the village must find them.

**What each player sees at the start:**
- **Wolf:** "You are the WOLF 🐺. Your wolf partners: Fahad."
- **العرّافة (Seer):** "Every night, pick one person and the app tells you their true role."
- **الحارس (Guard):** "Every night, protect one person from the wolves (not the same person twice in a row)."
- **الصيّاد (Hunter):** "If you die, you take one person with you."
- **القروي (Villager):** "No power. Find the wolves by talking and voting."

**How a round goes:** the same night → morning → talk → vote loop as Mafia. Villagers win when every wolf is out. Wolves win when they equal the villagers.

**How it differs from Mafia:** the Seer sees the **exact role** (Mafia's Detective only sees Mafia or not), there's a Hunter, and the names and art are Saudi (majlis, desert at night, a wolf howl sound).

### 6. برا السالفة (Out of the Loop) — 3 to 20 players
**In one line:** Everyone knows the secret topic except one person, who must pretend to know it.

**What each player sees at the start:**
- **Inside the topic:** "The topic is **كبسة 🍛** (category: Food). One person here doesn't know it. Find them!"
- **Out of the loop:** "You are **برا السالفة** 🤫. You don't know the topic. The category is: Food. Blend in, and try to guess it."

**How a round goes:**
1. The app picks who asks whom. Each player asks another player one question about the topic ("Do you eat it hot or cold?").
2. Answer without giving it away. Too vague looks suspicious, too clear helps the outsider.
3. After 2 rounds of questions, everyone votes for who they think is out of the loop.
4. If they catch the outsider, the outsider gets one last chance: pick the topic from 6 options. A correct pick wins anyway!
5. **Points:** you voted correctly = 1 · the outsider escapes = 3 · the outsider guesses the topic = 2.

**Categories:** food, places in Saudi, football clubs, animals, cars, jobs, series, and more.

### 7. سين جيم (Question & Answer) — 2 teams, any number of players
**In one line:** A trivia board: 6 categories × 3 questions each (200 / 400 / 600 points).

**What each player sees at the start:** "You are on **Team 1** (Ahmed, Sara, Majed)" and the board of 6 categories.

**How a round goes:**
1. The team whose turn it is picks a category and a point value.
2. The question appears on every phone, with a 60-second timer and the team discussing.
3. The team answers. Right = they get the points. Wrong = the other team can steal the points.
4. **Helpers (each team gets each one once):**
   - **اتصل بصديق (Call a friend):** call someone outside the game for 30 s.
   - **جاوب جوابين (Two answers):** say two answers, and either one counts.
   - **الحفرة (The Pit):** pick it BEFORE seeing the question. If you answer right, the other team **loses** those points.
5. When the board is empty, the highest score wins.

### 8. حزورة (Riddle) — 2 to 20 players
**In one line:** The app shows a riddle, and everyone types the answer privately. The fastest correct answer wins.

**What each player sees at the start:** "Riddles will appear. Type your answer. Faster = more points."

**How a round goes:**
1. A riddle appears ("I have teeth but I don't bite. What am I?"), with a 30-second timer.
2. Everyone types an answer. Nobody sees anyone else's.
3. Reveal: the correct answer is shown. 1st correct = 3 points · 2nd = 2 · others correct = 1.
4. 10 riddles per game. A leaderboard is shown at the end.

## Screens (sitemap)
Home (Create / Join) → Lobby (code, player list, Accept/Reject, mode) → Category → Game picker → How to play (each phone) → Game screens → Results / Play again.

## Security (built in from day 1)
- Roles and secret words are decided on the **server**. Each phone receives **only its own secret**, so a player can't find the Mafia by peeking at the app's network traffic.
- Every action (kill, save, vote) is checked on the server: only alive players with the correct role can do it, and only once per phase.
- Room codes expire when the room closes. The host must accept every join, and joining has a rate limit so nobody can guess codes.
- No accounts needed (just a name). Nothing personal is stored.

## MVP order
1. Rooms + code + host approval + How-to-play cards
2. برا السالفة (easiest; it proves the room system works)
3. المافيا + الذيب (one shared engine, two role sets)
4. أتوبيس كومبليت, حزورة, صراحة
5. سين جيم + كلمة سر (need big question banks)

## Decisions (2026-11-17)
- **Real app, NOT a web app.** Android = APK file (tap → install). iPhone = one-tap install (see below).
- **Arabic only.** Egyptian dialect in the Egyptian games, Saudi dialect in the Saudi games.
- **Built-in voice** in "everyone at home" mode (LiveKit). Mafia and wolves can talk only to each other at night, and everyone is unmuted in the morning.

## Tech plan
- App: Expo (React Native), one codebase → Android APK (EAS build) + iPhone home-screen web app (Expo web export, hosted on Cloudflare Pages).
- Rooms, roles, votes: Supabase (Realtime + Postgres + database functions). Secrets are created on the server and each phone reads only its own row.
- Voice: LiveKit Cloud (free tier to start). Separate voice channels for night (mafia/wolves) and day (everyone).
- Arabic right-to-left layout everywhere.
